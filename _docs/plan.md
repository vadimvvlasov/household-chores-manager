# Household Chores Manager – Project Scope (v1)

## Overview
A simple web app that gives a family of 4 a clear, shared system for household chores.  
Primary goal: limit total chore time so everyone has reliable free time for hobbies.

## Users
- 2 adults
- 2 children (ages 12–16)
- Shared family login (no individual accounts)

## Core Features

### Assignments & Planning
- Fixed weekly chore assignments
- Week starts on **Sunday**
- Daily checklists generated from the weekly plan
- Daily and weekly time budgets per person
- In-app calendar that shows **only chore time blocks**
- Instant chore swaps between any family members
- Both adults and kids can propose or edit assignments and time budgets
- Changes proposed by kids require adult approval before becoming active

### Tracking
- Simple check-off when a chore is done
- Log actual time spent on each chore
- Warning only (no blocking) if someone exceeds their daily or weekly time budget
- Full visibility: everyone can see everyone else’s completion status and time logs
- History of previous weeks is kept (who did what and how much time was spent)

### Notifications
- Notification before a scheduled time block starts
- Notification for unfinished daily chores

## Out of Scope for v1
- Points, streaks, or rewards system
- External calendar sync (Google, Apple, Outlook, etc.)
- Individual user accounts
- Photo proof or other verification beyond check-off + time log
- Complex approval workflows for swaps (swaps are instant)

## Success Criteria
The family has a single, clear place to see:
- What needs to be done today and this week
- Who is responsible
- How much time is budgeted vs actually spent
- When free time begins
